USE OF ENCRYPTION
-----------------
The app uses standard TLS 1.3 encryption provided by Apple's Network.framework
and Security.framework (no custom encryption implementations).
VLESS is a proxy protocol; the actual encryption is TLS 1.3.
We use CryptoKit only for the x25519 key exchange portion of the Reality handshake,
which is a standard Diffie-Hellman key agreement.

VPN ENTITLEMENT
---------------
The app uses the Network Extension entitlement (packet-tunnel-provider)
to create a VPN tunnel via NEPacketTunnelProvider.
This is standard iOS VPN functionality, identical to apps like WireGuard, Shadowrocket, etc.

PRIVACY
-------
The app does not track users. No analytics SDKs beyond RevenueCat (for subscription management).
PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy is included in the bundle with accurate declarations.
